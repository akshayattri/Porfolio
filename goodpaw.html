Design process / Building the connection

Start with a relationship.
Design the steps around it.

The design rationale moves from parent needs to a clear connection flow, then expands into everyday community and care.

01
Frame the needs

Bring discovery, familiarity, and everyday support together.

The product has three different jobs. Giving each one a clear home avoids asking a single feed to serve every intention.

Decision / distinct product destinations
02
Shape the core path

Map → profile → request → conversation.

Keep each step focused on one decision. Proximity starts discovery, profile context supports consideration, and mutual acceptance establishes the connection.

Decision / progressive commitment
03
Design for confidence

Explain boundaries at the moment they matter.

Place approximate-location guidance beside discovery, distinguish request states, and label owner-provided information clearly.

Decision / contextual guidance
04
Extend with purpose

Add familiarity and practical value.

Moments creates room for everyday sharing. Pet Care groups records and reminders without overwhelming the social profile. Shared colors, rounded cards, and familiar controls tie the spaces together.

Decision / separate contexts, shared visual language
01

Nearby.
Without being exact.

The map makes proximity tangible. Dog clusters, park markers, a radius control, and a draggable results drawer give discovery a spatial starting point.

Make the privacy boundary visible.

The interface states that locations are approximate beside the discovery controls. This puts the boundary where the location decision happens.

Pair exploration with browsing.

The results drawer provides a list-oriented route from the same map. Users can explore an area and then move into nearby profiles.

Give discovery an explicit state.

“Discovery on” makes visibility a recognizable setting rather than an invisible assumption.

02 / From discovery to connection

A pet’s profile.
A person behind it.

I paired pet identity with parent context, then connected that profile to friendship and messaging. This gives the path from browsing to a conversation a clear structure.

03 / Everyday community

Little moments.
Less pressure.

Moments gives everyday pet activity its own destination. The composer puts the photo first, follows with a short caption, and offers optional activity tags.

Make the sharing boundary explicit.

The captured composer explains the 24-hour lifetime and approximate location before a post is shared.

Keep the first contribution approachable.

A quiet empty state invites a first post rather than presenting an unexplained blank feed.

Separate community from coordination.

Moments, Friends, and Messages have different navigation destinations, giving public sharing and direct conversations distinct contexts.

04 / Everyday utility

Connection is social.
Care is practical.

I grouped health records, activity, and reminders under the pet’s care space. The dashboard provides recognizable entry points without putting every record into the profile itself.

Care Circle extends that space to trusted connections. The request is addressed to one person, with a defined time window and type of help.

The product boundary: trusted help, not an open marketplace.

Care requests go to an existing connection. Private conversations support handover details and routine-specific coordination.

05 / UI design system

Warm by character.
Consistent by design.

A cream canvas, dark ink, coral accents, and green actions create a warm, consistent system across discovery, community, and care.
