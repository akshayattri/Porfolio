01 / THE BRIEF

From a long inspection report
to a clearer way forward.

Home inspections are full of useful detail. But after the report arrives, the next steps can feel fragmented: what matters most, who should take action, and how does everything stay organized?

THE OPPORTUNITY

Design a calm, actionable workspace that helps homeowners move from understanding an issue to deciding what happens next.

THE EXPERIENCE

A connected product journey across properties, inspection findings, action planning and context-aware assistance.

THE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Provide useful context and clear priorities without presenting software as a substitute for professional judgment.

THE DESIGN QUESTION

How might we make an inspection feel less like a list of problems—and more like a plan?

HOMEWISE / CONCEPT EXPLORATION↘
02 / DISCOVERY

Understand the person
behind the property.

The experience was framed around two plausible situations: navigating an inspection for the first time, and managing ongoing care for a home already owned.

PROVISIONAL PERSONA / 0101

The new homeowner

“I know what the report says. I’m not sure what to do with it.”

WANTS TO

Understand unfamiliar terms and feel prepared for the next conversation with a qualified professional.

DESIGN IMPLICATION

Plain-language context, visible source evidence and priority explanations.

PROVISIONAL PERSONA / 0202

The active homeowner

“There are always a few things to fix. I need to keep track.”

WANTS TO

Keep important property details in one place and see what still needs follow-up.

DESIGN IMPLICATION

Connected findings, a persistent action planner and simple status management.

Research note. These are exploration personas and hypotheses, not fabricated user interviews or validated research findings. The concept calls for formal research and usability testing before production.

10 / REFLECTION

Less uncertainty.
More considered decisions.

Homewise explores a simple proposition: help people turn complex information about their homes into understandable next steps and an organized follow-through.

01

Let the report remain the source of truth, even when simplifying the language around it.

02

Connect findings to tasks so people don’t have to reconstruct the next step for themselves.

03

Use visual restraint to keep urgency and information density in balance.

This is an independent product concept, not a live customer deployment. Research findings, usability metrics and business impact have not been claimed without validation.
